# 20 — Roadmap

## Purpose
Provide a phased strategic roadmap from launch through long-term global category leadership.

## Objectives
- Sequence initiatives for maximum trust, growth, and monetization compounding.
- Align product, engineering, growth, and operations.
- Maintain adaptability through measurable milestones.

## Requirements
- Time-based phases with clear outcomes and dependencies.
- KPI-linked milestones and exit criteria.
- Risk-adjusted contingency plans.

## Architecture
- Phase A (Now → Pre-Q4): Launch core intelligence platform.
- Phase B (Q4 + 2 Quarters): Scale retention, automation, and SEO depth.
- Phase C (Year 2–3): Regional expansion and partner ecosystem.
- Phase D (Year 4+): Platformization, API ecosystem, and AI leadership.

## User Flow
1. Initial users adopt core decision tools.
2. Retention loops (alerts, wishlist, email, community) deepen engagement.
3. Global users receive localized intelligence and offers.
4. Ecosystem partners integrate platform intelligence APIs.

## Best Practices
- Review roadmap quarterly against KPI outcomes.
- Keep 70/20/10 allocation (core/growth/innovation).
- Use kill criteria for low-performing initiatives.

## Future Scalability
- Expansion by category, region, and channel.
- Marketplace-style partner integration layer.
- Autonomous optimization systems with human governance.

## Risks
- Roadmap bloat and execution dilution.
- External shocks (search policy, partner terms, regulation).
- Team scaling outpacing process maturity.

## KPIs
- Milestone completion confidence index.
- Revenue, retention, and trust score trendlines.
- International adoption and localization performance.

## Implementation Priority
1. Pre-Q4 launch and reliability.
2. Q4/Q1 growth and retention compounding.
3. Globalization and ecosystem expansion.

## Technical Notes
- Tie every roadmap milestone to a measurable north-star impact.
- Maintain dependency map and critical-path monitoring.
