# 17 — Q4 Launch Plan

## Purpose
Provide a launch execution blueprint for pre-Q4 readiness, controlled rollout, and high-confidence market entry.

## Objectives
- Launch core intelligence platform before Q4 shopping peak.
- Ensure reliability, trust, and conversion readiness.
- Establish incident and response playbooks.

## Requirements
- Defined MVP scope and freeze criteria.
- Launch command center with cross-functional ownership.
- Rollback and disaster recovery procedures.

## Architecture
- Phases:
  - Phase 0: Internal alpha and data validation.
  - Phase 1: Private beta with invite cohorts.
  - Phase 2: Public launch with regional traffic ramp.
  - Phase 3: Post-launch optimization.
- Readiness gates:
  - Data quality, AI safety, SEO indexation, affiliate reconciliation.

## User Flow
1. Target users onboard via waitlist/invite.
2. Core flows tested: discovery, comparison, alerts, conversion.
3. Feedback integrated in rapid release cycles.
4. Public launch expands channel and geographic reach.

## Best Practices
- Run chaos drills and failover exercises pre-launch.
- Use progressive traffic ramps with observability thresholds.
- Keep daily launch scorecard for all core KPIs.

## Future Scalability
- Regional launch kits and localization templates.
- Repeatable GTM playbook for new categories/markets.
- Continuous launch readiness framework.

## Risks
- Data feed volatility near holiday demand spikes.
- SEO volatility during index ramp.
- Alert system overload during major sales events.

## KPIs
- Launch stability SLA.
- Conversion rate from new users.
- 30-day retention for launch cohorts.

## Implementation Priority
1. Must-have core flows and reliability.
2. Growth channel activation.
3. Post-launch optimization waves.

## Technical Notes
- Freeze critical schemas two weeks pre-launch.
- Enable kill switches for AI and monetization modules.
