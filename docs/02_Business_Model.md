# 02 — Business Model

## Purpose
Define a sustainable, trust-first commercial model balancing user value, partner value, and long-term brand equity.

## Objectives
- Monetize without degrading recommendation quality.
- Diversify revenue beyond affiliate commissions.
- Align incentives around savings quality and purchase confidence.

## Requirements
- Affiliate network integrations with transparent disclosure.
- Merchant direct partnerships and premium placements governance.
- Subscription-ready architecture for advanced intelligence features.

## Architecture
- Revenue streams:
  - Affiliate CPA/CPS/CPC
  - Sponsored intelligence modules (clearly labeled)
  - Premium user tier (pro alerts, advanced comparisons)
  - B2B intelligence APIs
- Governance:
  - Strict wall between ranking integrity and sponsorship.

## User Flow
1. User receives recommendations ranked by value confidence.
2. Monetized opportunities are labeled and policy-checked.
3. User converts; attribution and commission pipeline processes event.
4. Revenue and quality signals feed optimization dashboard.

## Best Practices
- Enforce consistent “why this recommendation” disclosures.
- Cap sponsored impression share by category and session.
- Maintain merchant scorecards for fraud/refund rates.

## Future Scalability
- Regional affiliate hubs.
- Multi-currency, multi-tax operational reporting.
- Enterprise licensing of pricing intelligence.

## Risks
- Over-monetization harming trust metrics.
- Network tracking loss due to browser/privacy changes.
- Single-network payout risk.

## KPIs
- Revenue per qualified session.
- Net margin after content + infra + refund clawbacks.
- Sponsored CTR vs trust impact delta.

## Implementation Priority
1. Core affiliate revenue with policy guardrails.
2. Merchant direct partnerships.
3. Subscription and B2B API monetization.

## Technical Notes
- Attribution must support server-to-server fallback.
- Add consent-aware tracking logic by jurisdiction.
