# 04 — Brand Guidelines

## Purpose
Establish a trust-centric brand system that positions the platform as shopping intelligence, not discount noise.

## Objectives
- Build recognition around clarity, confidence, and evidence.
- Maintain consistency across product, content, email, and social.
- Reinforce credibility in every monetized touchpoint.

## Requirements
- Brand voice framework and editorial policy.
- Visual system for trust indicators and deal confidence.
- Disclosure standards for affiliate/sponsored content.

## Architecture
- Brand pillars: Transparent, Reliable, Human-Centered, Data-Backed.
- Voice model:
  - Default: concise and evidence-led.
  - Advisory: comparative and contextual.
  - Community: collaborative and respectful.

## User Flow
1. User encounters deal insight with confidence and source context.
2. Brand voice explains recommendation and trade-offs.
3. User sees clear disclosures and policy links.
4. Trust reinforced through consistency across channels.

## Best Practices
- Use plain language over hype.
- Always disclose ranking factors and partner relationships.
- Maintain editorial independence in guide/review content.

## Future Scalability
- Localization style guides per region.
- Brand governance playbook for partners/influencers.
- Audio/video brand expression standards.

## Risks
- Inconsistent messaging across teams/tools.
- Aggressive growth campaigns diluting trust tone.
- Regulatory changes in disclosure requirements.

## KPIs
- Brand trust score.
- Direct traffic growth.
- Content credibility ratings and complaint rates.

## Implementation Priority
1. Voice, disclosure, and trust signal standards.
2. Visual language and reusable templates.
3. Localization and partner governance.

## Technical Notes
- Include brand policy checks in CMS workflows.
- Track disclosure render events for compliance audits.
